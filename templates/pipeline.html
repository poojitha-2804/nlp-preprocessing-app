                        Continuous Bag of Words (CBOW)
                    

                    

                        Predicts the target center word given its surrounding context tokens. Several times faster to train, with superior representation for high-frequency words.
                    

                    

                        Formula: v_hidden = (1 / 2C) * Σ v_context

                        Objective: Maximize log P(w_target | Context)
                    

                

            

            

                

                    

                        Skip-Gram Model (SGNS)
                    

                    

                        Predicts the surrounding context words given a single center target word. Excels with small training corpora and preserves fine-grained semantic subtleties for rare words.
                    

                    

                        Formula: (w_target → w_{target+j}) for j ∈ [-C, +C]

                        Objective: Maximize Σ log P(w_context | w_target)
