Lemmatization Methods

Unlike stemming, lemmatization uses vocabulary and morphological context to convert words into valid canonical dictionary headwords (lemmas).

6 Lemmatization Approaches

Method 1
Dictionary-Based Lemmatization

Uses pre-compiled lookup tables mapping inflected words directly to canonical dictionary headwords.

Example: "children" → "child", "mice" → "mouse"
Method 2
Rule-Based Lemmatization

Combines morphological inflection rules with exception lists to deduce dictionary base forms.

Example: "cities" → "city", "wolves" → "wolf"
Method 3
WordNet Lemmatization

Powered by Princeton's WordNet lexical database. Default mapping assumes Noun unless POS tag specified.

Example: "cars" → "car", "running" → "running" (default Noun)
Method 4
POS-Based Lemmatization

Tags Part-of-Speech (Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb) before lemmatizing. Transforms verb `running` → `run` accurately.

Example: "running" (Verb) → "run", "better" (Adjective) → "good"
Method 5
Context-Aware Lemmatization

Analyzes sentence structure and dependency syntax to disambiguate words that act as different POS in context.

Example: "meeting" in "We are meeting" → "meet"
Method 6
Transformer/Contextual Lemmatization

Uses deep neural network embeddings (spaCy/Transformers) to assign contextually perfect lemmas for complex sentences.

Example: "The meeting was organized" → "meeting" (Noun)
