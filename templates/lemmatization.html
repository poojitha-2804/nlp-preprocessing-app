Lemmatization & POS Approaches

Method 1 (Primary)
POS-Based Lemmatization

Analyzes Part-Of-Speech tags (Determiner, Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb) before looking up lemmas. Transforms verb running → run, is → be accurately.

Input: "this is a book"
POS Tags: ["this/DT", "is/VBZ", "a/DT", "book/NN"]
Lemmas: ["this", "be", "a", "book"]
Method 2
WordNet Standard Lemmatization

Utilizes Princeton's WordNet lexical database. Default mapping assumes Noun unless POS tag specified.

Example: "cars" → "car", "running" → "running" (default Noun)
Method 3
Context-Aware & Transformer POS Lemmatization

Uses spaCy deep neural network embeddings to assign contextually perfect lemmas for complex sentences.

Example: "The meeting was organized" → "meeting" (Noun)
Method 4
Dictionary-Based Lemmatization

Uses pre-compiled lookup tables mapping inflected words directly to canonical dictionary headwords.

Example: "children" → "child", "mice" → "mouse"
Method 5
Rule-Based Morphological Lemmatization

Combines morphological inflection rules with exception lists to deduce dictionary base forms.

Example: "cities" → "city", "wolves" → "wolf"
