10 Cleaning & Normalization Operations

1. Lowercasing

Converts all text characters to lowercase so `Word` and `word` map to identical tokens.

2. Uppercase Conversion

Converts characters to uppercase for specific keyword extraction or matching tasks.

3. Punctuation Removal

Strips punctuation marks (`!`, `?`, `.`, `,`, `;`) from tokens.

4. Number Removal

Removes digits (`0-9`) when numerical values are irrelevant to semantic intent.

5. Special Character Removal

Strips symbols (`@`, `#`, `$`, `%`, `^`, `&`, `*`) keeping strictly alphanumeric text.

6. Extra Whitespace Removal

Collapses multiple spaces, tabs, and newlines into a clean single space format.

7. HTML Tag Removal

Parses and strips HTML markup (e.g. `<div>`, `<p>`) from web scrapes.

8. URL Removal

Strips web hyperlinks (`http://`, `https://`, `www.`) from text streams.

9. Emoji Handling

Strips or normalizes emoji symbols (`😊`, `🚀`) from social media comments.

10. Contraction Expansion

Expands informal English contractions (`can't` → `cannot`, `won't` → `will not`).
